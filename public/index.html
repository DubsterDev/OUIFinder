Super Fast OUI Manufacturer Finder

The fastest OUI finder on the internet. Gives you the owner of any OUI in an instant.

Examples:

  • 000D72: A line with just six characters (only works on its own line)
  • abcd.0123.a312: A line with a mac address with three parts seperated by dots or dashes. Can be anywhere in the line.
  • 00:12:34:56:12:76: A line with a mac address with six parts seperated by colons or dashes. Can be anywhere in the line.

Also, the same format does not need to be used in every line. Every format mentioned above, expect for the first one, works anywhere in a line.

Data last fetched from standards-oui.ieee.org on October 26, 2026
